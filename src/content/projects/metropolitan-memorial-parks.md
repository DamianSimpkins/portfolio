Metropolitan Memorial Parks cares for some of Sydney's best-known cemeteries and memorial parks, including Rookwood, Field of Mars, Eastern Suburbs, Frenchs Forest and Gore Hill. People arrive at its website at a difficult time, often needing one thing quickly: a service time, a loved one's resting place, or the first steps in planning a funeral. I designed the website around those moments, with a calm, warm look, plain language and clear paths to the most common tasks, so families can find what they need without having to search for it.
