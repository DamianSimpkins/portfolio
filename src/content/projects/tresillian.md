sections:
  - label: The age-stage system
    text: |-
      Advice is organised by a child's age, and each stage has its own colour and icon set: Newborn, Baby, Toddler and Parent. The age pages share one structure, a coloured hero, icon tiles for the main topics, then the content, so a parent always knows where they are. Pages are short, in plain language, with large touch targets and one clear next step. The icon set is fully custom, drawn for each topic and tinted to its stage.
  - label: Booking a visit
    before: 4
    text: |-
      I made it easier for parents to get help and book a visit. The booking process now gives people as much detail as possible up front, and once they've booked they see clear next steps, including details of the location they've chosen. With that information on hand, fewer parents need to call after booking.
  - label: Sponsorship
    before: 5
    text: |-
      Growing sponsorship was another business goal. On the old site sponsor slots felt like paid advertising and often repeated the same ad on every page. I designed sponsored elements that sit inline with the page and site content, so they read as part of the page and not as an interruption.
  - label: Outcome
    before: 6
    text: |-
      Giving parents next steps and location details the moment they book cut the calls Tresillian's team take from people who have already booked.

[Tresillian](https://www.tresillian.org.au/) is Australia's largest not-for-profit early parenting organisation, supporting families with newborns, babies and toddlers through online guides, courses, day services and residential stays. I led design on its new website, from research through to the finished templates.
