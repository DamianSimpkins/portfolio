Inside the Sydney Harbour Bridge's South-East Pylon, the BridgeMuseum continues the story that BridgeClimb starts above the harbour. Visitors explore the Bridge's design, engineering, construction and cultural significance at their own pace, through interactive displays, historic memorabilia and multimedia storytelling. I designed the website to bring that experience to life, with bold colour and photography, a clear structure, and a simple way to book tickets.
